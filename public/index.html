How it works

From a question to a cited answer.

Every message goes through the same five steps. The first one never calls a model, which is why answers start fast.

    Memory

    Everything you asked, by date.

    After each answer I write down the checkable facts — prices, releases, what you told me. Ask “has it changed?” and I compare against exactly these.

    Facts I'm tracking

      Facts are recorded with the date I said them. “Re-check” opens a new chat that compares them against today's web.

      About the data

      What I use, and what I can't see.

      Sources

      • Web search: live results with publish dates. Each claim is cited with a numbered source.
      • Your history: every chat, stored locally on this machine with its date.
      • The model's own knowledge: used only for stable facts, and flagged when it could be stale.

      Limitations

      • Search passages can lag the real page, especially pricing tables.
      • Paywalled or JavaScript-only pages may not be readable.
      • Memory lives on this computer only; clearing data/ erases it.
